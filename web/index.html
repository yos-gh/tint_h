TINT horizontal is a physics-driven falling-block puzzle. Keep soft, connected stones near the center of a curved bowl and clear them in vertical columns.

How to play

Goal

Build a continuous vertical column from the green line down to the curved floor to clear it. Slightly winding columns still count.

Scoring

Clear several columns together, or within one second of each other, to raise the chain multiplier.

Game over

The game ends when the pile stays beyond either red deadline for five continuous seconds.
