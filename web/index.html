TINT HORIZONTAL

keep it centered
Source

TINT horizontal is a physics-driven falling-block puzzle. Keep soft, connected stones near the center of a curved bowl, build vertical columns, and clear them before the pile crosses either deadline.

Controls

  • Move: A / D or arrow keys
  • Soft drop: S or down arrow
  • Rotate: N / M
  • Restart: R
  • Gamepad and touchscreen supported

Clear Rule

Build a continuous vertical column from the green line to the curved floor. Slightly winding columns still count.

Deadlines

Stones may cross a red deadline briefly. Keep the pile beyond either line for five continuous seconds and the game ends.

Scoring

Clear multiple columns together—or within one second—to increase the chain multiplier.
